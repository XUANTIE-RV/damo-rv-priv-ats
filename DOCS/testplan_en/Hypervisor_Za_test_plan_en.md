# Hypervisor × Za Atomic Extensions Cross Test Plan

> This document describes the test plan for cross-scenarios between the Hypervisor (H) extension and the Za-series atomic/reservation-set extensions. This plan was split out from `Hypervisor_Zi_test_plan.md`: the former Group 8 (Hypervisor × Zalrsc) and the former Group 4 (Hypervisor × Zawrs) were migrated in entirely, with **test case IDs unchanged** (HZLRSC-01~40, HZWRS-01~12) and Group numbers rearranged within this subset. The Hypervisor × Zaamo cross group (HZAMO-01~31, Group 2) was supplemented afterwards, and the former Zawrs group number was correspondingly adjusted from Group 2 to Group 3. The Hypervisor × Zacas cross group (HZACAS-01~36, Group 3) was then supplemented, and the former Zawrs group number was correspondingly adjusted from Group 3 to Group 4. The Hypervisor × Zabha cross group (HZABHA-01~39, Group 4) was then supplemented, and the former Zawrs group number was correspondingly adjusted from Group 4 to Group 5. This revision supplements the Hypervisor × Zalasr cross group (HZLASR-01~36, Group 5), and the former Zawrs group number was correspondingly adjusted from Group 5 to Group 6. In addition: following the principle that "all virtualization cases belong to this plan", `Zacas_test_plan.md` has removed the HS/VS/VU-mode cases of its Group 11 (the ZACAS-50/51/52 IDs are retired and no longer reused, taken over by HZACAS-01/02/03 of this group), and the two dimensions of the former ZACAS-53 (Smstateen does not gate amocas) that belong to V=1 scenarios — "zeroing `hstateen0`" and "execution in VS/VU-mode" — were migrated into this group as the newly added HZACAS-36 (Group 3.8); `Zacas_test_plan.md` retains only the M/S/U non-virtualization perspective of ZACAS-53.
>
> The Group order follows extension dependency relationships: Zalrsc defines the reservation set and the LR/SC atomic instructions (Group 1); Zaamo defines the AMO atomic read-modify-write instructions, is a subset of the A extension together with Zalrsc (sharing opcode 0x2F), and shares the store/AMO exception classification and the transformed atomic instruction rules (Group 2); Zacas defines the compare-and-swap instructions of the AMO family (`amocas.w/d/q`), depends on Zaamo (`zacas.adoc`: "The Zacas extension depends upon the Zaamo extension"), and is likewise classified under the store/AMO exception class, but the "compare" step of CAS makes its memory write conditional (similar to SC, unlike the unconditional write of other AMOs) (Group 3); Zabha extends the AMO family to byte/halfword widths (`amoadd.b/h` etc., 9 operations × `.b`/`.h` = 18 instructions, funct3=000/001, plus `amocas.b/h` under the Zacas condition), depends on Zaamo (`amocas.b/h` additionally depends on Zacas), shares the store/AMO exception classification and the transformed atomic instruction rules, but the 1-byte alignment of byte AMOs always holds (never misaligned, no MAG branch), the misaligned/MAG relaxation is testable only for halfword AMOs, and the sign-extension of rd to 8/16 bits and the ignoring of the upper bits of rs2/rd are its distinctive data semantics (Group 4); Zalasr provides **independent atomic ordered pure loads (load-acquire, `lb/lh/lw/ld.aq`, funct5=00110) and pure stores (store-release, `sb/sh/sw/sd.rl`, funct5=00111)**, builds on Zaamo/Zalrsc/Zabha but can be implemented independently (`norm:zalasr_builds_on_amo`), shares opcode 0x2F and the transformed atomic instruction rules, but because they are pure loads/pure stores (not read-modify-write) the exception classification is **split into the load class (load-acquire, cause 4/5/13/21) and the store/AMO class (store-release, cause 6/7/15/23)** — isomorphic to but mechanistically different from the LR/SC split of Group 1 Zalrsc (Zalrsc is the two halves of a paired read-modify-write, Zalasr is two independent instructions), and load-acquire requires only read permission while store-release always writes, making the D-bit requirement mandatory (Group 5); the `wrs.nto`/`wrs.sto` semantics of Zawrs build on the reservation set established by LR (Group 6).
>
> Split date: 2026-09-03

an implicit VS-stage walk fault reports per the original type (load-acquire → cause 21, store-release → cause 23, `norm:H_vm_gpapriv`); disambiguation in `htinst` between the transformed atomic instruction (**uniformly using the transformedatomicinst format of opcode 0x2F rather than transformedload/storeinst**, retaining funct5=00110/00111, aq/rl, funct3, rd/rs2) and the pseudoinstruction; the `hstatus.GVA`/`SPV` combination of guest Zalasr traps; the difference in `htval` values; the MAG relaxation branch of misaligned load-acquire/store-release; **store-release always writes, so a missing D bit when ADUE=0 reports a store page-fault (mandatory, same as Zaamo)**, whereas load-acquire is a pure read involving only the A bit (not setting D); the ordering modification of `henvcfg.FIOM` on load-acquire/store-release that **always carry aq/rl** (more universal than the optional aq/rl of LR/SC/AMO); **reserved encodings (a load without aq / a store without rl, including load-release/store-acquire) report illegal-instruction (cause=2) rather than cause=22 at V=1** (`norm:H_cause_virtual_instruction`: a reserved encoding is not HS-qualified, same as HZABHA-37 in Group 4); the architectural boundary that the H extension has no atomic ordered load/store virtual-machine equivalent but HLV/HSV can replicate the data transfer (losing atomicity and RCsc ordering)
- **Hypervisor × Zawrs**: normal execution of `wrs.nto`/`wrs.sto` in HS/VS/VU-mode (must not spuriously raise a virtual-instruction exception); `hstatus.VTW` virtual-instruction gating of `wrs.nto` in VS/VU-mode; exception type determination with `mstatus.TW` taking precedence over `hstatus.VTW`; the VTW clause applies only to `wrs.nto` and not to `wrs.sto`; trap reporting of the VTW exception (stval/SPV)

## Group 1. Hypervisor × Zalrsc Cross Tests

**Intersection points with the Hypervisor**:
1. **Exception-class split**: LR is a load access → load class cause 4/5/13/21; SC is a store access → store/AMO class cause 6/7/15/23. Authoritative basis: `norm:mcause_exccode_st_sc_amo` (store-conditional generates store/AMO exceptions), `norm:load_page_fault_no_r` (load-reserved requires read permission), `norm:store_page_fault_no_w` (store-conditional requires write permission, and an unreadable page is necessarily unwritable so it reports the store class)
2. **Delegation-path split**: the `hedeleg` bits for VS-stage faults (cause 4/5/6/7/13/15) are Writable and can be delegated down to VS-mode; the `hedeleg` bits for G-stage faults (cause 21/23) are Read-only 0 and are **architecturally forced** to trap into HS-mode (`norm:hedeleg_acc`, `norm:H_vm_gpatrans`)
3. **htinst two-path disambiguation**: an explicit-access fault → transformed atomic instruction (retaining funct5/aq/rl/funct3/rd/rs2, only bits19:15 ← Addr. Offset) or 0; an implicit VS-stage page-table-walk fault → **must** be a pseudoinstruction (read 0x00003000 / A-D write 0x00003020, RV64) and 0 is not allowed. htinst is the **only means** by which HS-mode distinguishes the two (htval points to the target data GPA and the PTE GPA respectively)
4. **Addr. Offset always 0**: Addr. Offset = faulting VA − original VA, which can be non-zero only for a misaligned split access; but `norm:lr_sc_alignment` requires LR/SC to be naturally aligned with **no MAG relaxation** (unlike Zalasr), so a misaligned access raises an exception directly without splitting, hence the Addr. Offset of LR/SC is always 0
5. **GVA/SPV combination**: a guest LR/SC trap → GVA=1 and SPV=1; whereas an HLV/HLVX/HSV explicit-access fault → GVA=1 but SPV=0. The contrast proves that LR/SC are not virtual-machine memory access instructions
6. **A failed SC is still subject to the write-permission check**: `norm:sc_retire_permission` (shall, mandatory) + `norm:sc_failed_as_store` (may) → an SC that necessarily fails due to no reservation and logically does not write memory **must still** report a store-class exception when targeting a page without write permission (including cause=23 for G-stage)
7. **No HLR/HSC architectural boundary**: `norm:hlsv_op` maps only the RV32I/RV64I basic load/store; LR/SC (AMO opcode) have no virtual-machine equivalent → HS-mode cannot perform an atomic RMW on guest memory with VS/VU effective privilege (`norm:hlsv_priv`)
8. **cause=22 exclusion**: none of the virtual-instruction clauses in `hypervisor.adoc` (VTVM/VTW/VTSR, HLV/HSV/HLVX, CBO gating, counter CSRs, etc.) **covers LR/SC** → guest LR/SC never report cause=22
9. **henvcfg.FIOM × aq/rl**: when V=1 and FIOM=1, the ordering of LR/SC with aq/rl accessing a device-I/O-ordered region is modified (`norm:henvcfg_fiom_order`) — the only direct path by which the hypervisor can configurably modify Zalrsc semantics
10. **henvcfg.ADUE × failed-SC side effect**: `norm:sc_failed_side_effects` (UNSPECIFIED) combined with `norm:henvcfg_adue_op`, and the D bits of the two-level VS-stage/G-stage PTEs are **each independently** UNSPECIFIED (double uncertainty)
11. **Forward progress × virtualization trap**: `norm:constrained_lrsc_forward_progress_trap` ("H traps") means the guarantee is already satisfied when a guest constrained loop is preempted/interrupted by the hypervisor — the Zalrsc livelock-freedom guarantee is naturally compatible with virtualization
12. **Page-table memory RsrvEventual**: hardware A/D updates must perform an atomic RMW on the PTE, so the guest physical memory holding the VS-stage (`vsatp`) and G-stage (`hgatp`) page tables must both have the RsrvEventual PMA (`ptmem_rsrv_eventual`)

## Group 2. Hypervisor × Zaamo Cross Tests

**Intersection points with the Hypervisor**:
1. **Exceptions uniformly classified under the store/AMO class**: even though an AMO includes a read phase, all its exceptions are classified under the store/AMO class (misaligned=6, access fault=7, page fault=15, guest-page fault=23), **never** reporting the load class (4/5/13/21). Authoritative basis: `norm:mcause_exccode_st_sc_amo` (AMOs generate store/AMO exceptions) + the `norm:store_page_fault_no_w` accompanying NOTE ("AMOs never raise load page-fault exceptions"; an unreadable page is necessarily unwritable, so an AMO to an unreadable page always reports a store page-fault). This is the fundamental difference from LR (load class) in Group 1
2. **AMOs require R+W permission**: an AMO to an R=1/W=0 or R=0 page reports a store page-fault (15) (VS-stage) or store guest-page-fault (23) (G-stage); contrasted with LR requiring only read permission (HZLRSC-25)
3. **Delegation-path split**: the `hedeleg` bits for VS-stage AMO faults (cause 6/7/15) are Writable and can be delegated down to VS-mode; `hedeleg[23]` for G-stage AMO faults (cause 23) is Read-only 0 and **architecturally forced** to trap into HS-mode (`norm:hedeleg_acc`, `norm:H_vm_gpatrans`)
4. **An implicit VS-stage walk fault reports cause=23 per the original AMO type**: `norm:H_vm_gpapriv` — an implicit PTE access checks permission as an implicit load/store, but the exception is "always reported for the original access type", so the implicit-walk GPF of an AMO reports a store/AMO guest-page fault (cause=23), **not** the load guest-page fault of LR (cause=21, HZLRSC-19)
5. **htinst transformed atomic instruction**: an AMO explicit-access fault → `htinst`/`mtinst` = 0 or transformed atomic (retaining funct5/aq/rl/funct3/rd/rs2, only bits19:15 ← Addr. Offset); the `transformedatomicinst` format figure explicitly covers AMOs; the opcode 0x2F branch of the framework `hyp_transform_mem_inst()` is ready
6. **Addr. Offset always 0 (rationale differs from Zalrsc)**: AMO atomicity requires a single memory operation — under MAG relaxation it does not split across pages, and without relaxation it raises an exception directly, hence Addr. Offset is always 0 (Zalrsc has **no** MAG relaxation and raises an exception directly on misalignment; the rationale differs but the conclusion is the same)
7. **MAG relaxation (unique to Zaamo)**: `norm:misaligned_atomicity_granule_size` — a misaligned AMO whose bytes all lie within the same MAG granule raises no alignment exception and executes atomically; Zalrsc has no such branch
8. **GVA/SPV combination**: a guest AMO trap → GVA=1 and SPV=1 (SPVP=1 for a VS origin, SPVP=0 for a VU origin); contrasted with an HLV/HLVX/HSV explicit-access fault → GVA=1 but SPV=0
9. **An AMO always writes ⇒ the D-bit requirement is deterministic**: an AMO always performs a write-back, so when `henvcfg.ADUE`=0 it reports a store page-fault (Svade path) for a VS-stage page with A=0 or D=0, **not UNSPECIFIED** (unlike the double UNSPECIFIED PTE D-bit side effect of a failed SC in Group 1, HZLRSC-32/33)
10. **henvcfg.FIOM × aq/rl**: `norm:henvcfg_fiom_order` explicitly states "an atomic instruction that accesses a region ordered as device I/O has its aq and/or rl bit set"; when V=1 and FIOM=1, the ordering of an AMO with aq/rl accessing a device-I/O-ordered region is modified to constrain both I/O and memory
11. **cause=22 exclusion**: none of the virtual-instruction clauses in `hypervisor.adoc` covers AMOs → guest AMOs never report cause=22 (same as LR/SC)
12. **No AMO virtual-machine equivalent**: `norm:hlsv_op` maps only the RV32I/RV64I basic load/store; AMOs (opcode 0x2F) have no HL*/HS* equivalent → HS-mode cannot perform an atomic RMW on guest memory with VS/VU effective privilege (an architectural boundary shared with HZLRSC-39/40 of Group 1)

**Spec Reference**:
- `amo_rmw_semantics` / `norm:amo_operand_size`: the AMO atomic read-modify-write and width/sign-extension semantics are unchanged in VS/VU-mode (non-virtualized semantics are covered by `Zaamo_test_plan.md`; this group only verifies that virtualization does not change the semantics)
- `norm:mcause_exccode_st_sc_amo` / `norm:store_page_fault_no_w` / `norm:load_page_fault_no_r`: AMO exceptions are uniformly classified under the store/AMO class, require R+W permission, and never report a load page-fault
- `norm:hedeleg_acc` / `norm:hedeleg_op` / `norm:H_vm_gpatrans`: the delegation-path split and that a G-stage fault must report a store/AMO guest-page fault
- `norm:H_vm_gpapriv`: an implicit VS-stage walk fault reports cause=23 per the original AMO type (store/AMO)
- `norm:H_trap_xtinst_val` / `htinst_transformed_atomic` / `norm:H_trap_xtinst_guestpage` / `norm:H_trap_xtinst_guestpage_rw`: the two classes of htinst value rules — transformed and pseudoinstruction

- `norm:amo_alignment` / `norm:misaligned_atomicity_granule_size`: the exception path and MAG relaxation branch of a misaligned AMO

> [!NOTE]
> - All tests in this group must detect the H extension at runtime via `HAS_H_EXT()`; Zaamo/A support follows the platform configuration macro `ZAAMO_SUPPORTED` (or `A_SUPPORTED`), with a secondary probe by trap-armed execution of an AMO; if not satisfied, the whole group TEST_SKIP.
> - **Exception-class assertions must be precise and distinct from LR**: **all** AMO exceptions are classified under the store/AMO class (misaligned=6, access fault=7, page fault=15, guest-page fault=23), per `norm:mcause_exccode_st_sc_amo` and the `norm:store_page_fault_no_w` accompanying NOTE. Assertions must **never** accept the load class (4/5/13/21) — an AMO to an unreadable page also reports cause=15 rather than 13; the implicit VS-stage walk GPF of an AMO reports cause=23 rather than 21 (`norm:H_vm_gpapriv`). This is the fundamental divide from Group 1 (LR reports the load class).
> - **cause=22 is a mandatory negative assertion**: if guest AMOs in HZAMO-02/03 report a virtual-instruction exception, it violates the SPEC (no clause in `hypervisor.adoc` imposes virtual-instruction gating on AMOs); it must remain failed and be recorded in the `bugs/` directory, and the assertion must not be relaxed.
> - **htinst golden-value computation is ready**: the framework `hyp_transform_mem_inst()` for opcode 0x2F already implements "Atomic (LR/SC/AMO): retain all fields except bits19:15", matching `htinst_transformed_atomic` word-for-word; HZAMO-15~17 can reuse it directly; the implicit-walk scenarios of HZAMO-18/19 can reuse `setup_implicit_walk_victim()`. The aq/rl variants (HZAMO-16/25), if the framework has no corresponding primitive, must be injected with raw encoding or the primitive supplemented.
> - **Mandatory nature of the htinst pseudoinstruction**: in HZAMO-18, when `htval` is non-zero and the fault originates from a VS-stage implicit access, writing 0 to `htinst` violates `norm:H_trap_xtinst_guestpage` ("zero is not allowed") and must remain failed; in the explicit-access scenario of HZAMO-15, writing 0 to `htinst` is allowed.
> - **MAG relaxation is a branch unique to Zaamo**: HZAMO-22/23 depend on the platform declaring a misaligned atomicity granule (`norm:misaligned_atomicity_granule_size`); when MAG is not declared, a misaligned AMO must raise cause=6/7 (HZAMO-21) and HZAMO-22/23 SKIP. This contrasts with Zalrsc (HZLRSC-27/28, no MAG relaxation, misalignment always raises an exception). Misaligned exceptions allow two compliant results — address-misaligned and access-fault; the cases verify that the cause belongs to the expected set and record the actual value, and must not judge FAIL because the platform chose one of them.
> - **An AMO always writes ⇒ the D bit is mandatory**: in HZAMO-28 an AMO always performs a write-back, so reporting a store page-fault for a D=0 page when ADUE=0 is a **mandatory** result (not recording type); this is fundamentally different from the PTE D-bit side effect of a failed SC in Group 1 (HZLRSC-32/33, UNSPECIFIED recording type) and must not be confused.
> - **Recording-type cases must not be forcibly judged**: HZAMO-30/31 (the architectural boundary of having no AMO virtual-machine equivalent) are architecture-permitted/implementation-defined behaviors, recording only the implementation choice.
> - **Multi-hart limitation**: the FIOM ordering-effect assertions of HZAMO-25/26 and HZAMO-31 require secondary-hart support; the current common framework runs only hart 0, so the related assertions SKIP (not FAIL) until the framework is ready, and single-hart platforms likewise SKIP with a note.
> - **Permission-case dependencies**: 2.2/2.3/2.6 require VS-stage (`vsatp`) and G-stage (`hgatp`) page-table construction capability (mapping combinations such as R=0, R=1/W=0, A=0, D=0, and G-stage without write permission); the permission-check mechanism itself is covered by `pmp_test_plan.md`/`vm_test_plan.md`, and this group only verifies the permission-semantics conclusions of AMOs.
> - Zaamo non-virtualized semantics (AMO encoding, data semantics of the 9 operations, rd sign-extension, ignoring of the upper 32 bits of rs2, aq/rl data semantics, multi-hart atomicity, basic executability at each of the M/S/U privilege levels) are covered by `Zaamo_test_plan.md`; the main-memory AMOArithmetic-level PMA support guarantee is covered by `Ziccamoa_test_plan.md`, and this group does not duplicate them.

## Group 3. Hypervisor × Zacas Cross Tests

**Intersection points with the Hypervisor**:
1. **Exceptions uniformly classified under the store/AMO class**: `amocas.w/d/q` and the Zaamo AMOs belong to the same opcode 0x2F instruction family (funct5=00101); `norm:mcause_exccode_st_sc_amo` explicitly states "AMO instructions generate store/AMO exceptions", which covers amocas, so all its exceptions (misaligned=6, access fault=7, page fault=15, guest-page fault=23) **never** report the load class (4/5/13/21), consistent with the Zaamo conclusion in Group 2. This group does not re-derive the general classification logic and only re-verifies the same cause classification with the specific amocas instruction word (funct5=00101)
2. **Unconditional write-permission check (unique to Zacas, isomorphic to the failed SC of Group 1)**: `norm:Zacas_amocas_w_permission` ("always requires write permissions") is unconditional mandatory language; even when the CAS comparison fails (`zacas_failed_cas_write_note`: the implementation may choose not to write memory), the permission check must still be performed and a store-class exception reported — this is essentially isomorphic to `norm:sc_retire_permission`/`norm:sc_failed_as_store` in Group 1 (a failed SC is still subject to the write-permission check, HZLRSC-22~26), and differs from the Zaamo AMOs in Group 2 (which always write back unconditionally with no "failure" branch, so the "failure still requires a permission check" dimension need not be specifically verified)
3. **Delegation-path split**: the `hedeleg` bits for VS-stage amocas faults (cause 6/7/15) are Writable and can be delegated down to VS-mode; `hedeleg[23]` for G-stage amocas faults (cause 23) is Read-only 0 and **architecturally forced** to trap into HS-mode (`norm:hedeleg_acc`, `norm:H_vm_gpatrans`) — sharing the same delegation mechanism with Group 1/Group 2; this group probes independently to establish the premise (keeping each group self-contained without cross-group references to probe results)
4. **An implicit VS-stage walk fault reports cause=23 per the original amocas type**: `norm:H_vm_gpapriv` — an implicit PTE access checks permission as an implicit load/store, but the exception is "always reported for the original access type", so the implicit-walk GPF of amocas reports a store/AMO guest-page fault (cause=23), consistent with the Zaamo conclusion in Group 2 (HZAMO-18)
5. **htinst transformed atomic instruction**: an amocas explicit-access fault → `htinst`/`mtinst` = 0 or transformed atomic (retaining funct5=00101/funct3/rd/rs2/aq/rl, only bits19:15 ← Addr. Offset); the `transformedatomicinst` format figure explicitly covers "AMO instruction" (including amocas); the specific handling of funct5=00101 in the opcode 0x2F branch of the framework `hyp_transform_mem_inst()` must be verified (although it shares the same branch with amoadd etc., the golden-value computation must still be confirmed by measuring the actual amocas instruction word, not excluding the possibility that the framework refines handling by funct5)
6. **Addr. Offset always 0**: the natural-alignment requirement of amocas (`norm:Zacas_amocas_rs1_addr_alignment`, 4/8/16 bytes) and atomicity require a single memory operation, hence the htinst Addr. Offset is always 0 (same rationale as Group 1/Group 2)
7. **MAG relaxation (cross-referencing Zaamo)**: the "the same exception options apply" of `norm:Zacas_amocas_rs1_addr_alignment` explicitly points to the Zaamo alignment-exception options, so amocas likewise applies the MAG relaxation branch of `norm:misaligned_atomicity_granule_size` (unlike Group 1 Zalrsc which has no MAG relaxation)
8. **GVA/SPV combination**: a guest amocas trap → GVA=1 and SPV=1 (SPVP=1 for a VS origin, SPVP=0 for a VU origin); contrasted with an HLV/HLVX/HSV explicit-access fault → GVA=1 but SPV=0 (sharing the same conclusion with Group 1/Group 2; this group verifies independently to confirm that amocas is not a virtual-machine memory access instruction)
9. **A successful CAS always writes ⇒ the D-bit requirement is deterministic; the D-bit side effect of a failed CAS is a SPEC gap**: when the CAS comparison succeeds it always performs a write-back, so when `henvcfg.ADUE`=0 it reports a store page-fault (Svade path) for a VS-stage page with A=0 or D=0, **mandatory** (same as HZAMO-27/28 of Group 2); but when the CAS comparison **fails**, `zacas_failed_cas_write_note` allows the implementation to choose not to write memory, and the Zacas SPEC does **not** explicitly stipulate the PTE D-bit side effect in this scenario as UNSPECIFIED the way Zalrsc's `norm:sc_failed_side_effects` does — this is a SPEC gap, handled as recording type by this group (HZACAS-32/33) with the difference explicitly annotated, no mandatory verdict
10. **henvcfg.FIOM × aq/rl**: `norm:henvcfg_fiom_order` explicitly states "an atomic instruction that accesses a region ordered as device I/O has its aq and/or rl bit set", covering amocas (an atomic instruction); when V=1 and FIOM=1, the ordering of amocas with aq/rl accessing a device-I/O-ordered region is modified; additionally, `norm:Zacas_amocas_mem_op_fail_aq_rl` (no release semantics on the failure path regardless of rl) is a Zacas-specific rule; the ordering axiom itself requires multi-hart stress-test support, and this group only verifies executability and data semantics
11. **cause=22 exclusion**: none of the virtual-instruction clauses in `hypervisor.adoc` covers amocas (same as LR/SC/AMO) → guest amocas never reports cause=22
12. **No amocas virtual-machine equivalent**: `norm:hlsv_op` maps only the RV32I/RV64I basic load/store; amocas (opcode 0x2F, funct5=00101) has no HL*/HS* equivalent → HS-mode cannot perform an atomic CAS on guest memory with VS/VU effective privilege (an architectural-boundary conclusion shared with HZLRSC-39/40 of Group 1 and HZAMO-30/31 of Group 2)
13. **`hstateen0` does not gate amocas (V=1-side Smstateen non-gating)**: the gating trigger condition of `norm:stateen_illegal_state_access` is "executing an instruction that would **read or write the protected state**", and `norm:stateen_op` stipulates that `hstateen0` only controls VS/VU access to state. Zacas has no CSR and introduces no architectural state (using only general registers and memory operands), with no stateen bit assignable (`norm:stateen_unimplemented_state_roz` can corroborate this in reverse), so after zeroing all bits of `hstateen0`, VS/VU-mode execution of `amocas.w/d/q` must still complete normally and **never** report virtual-instruction (cause=22) or illegal-instruction (cause=2). This is an independent causal branch of the cause=22 exclusion (intersection point 11): HZACAS-02/03 verify that "no clause in `hypervisor.adoc` imposes virtual-instruction gating on amocas", while HZACAS-36 verifies that "even if the hypervisor actively zeroes `hstateen0` to try to close state access, amocas is not thereby gated"; the two rest on different bases and are not interchangeable

> - All tests in this group must detect the H extension at runtime via `HAS_H_EXT()`; Zacas support follows the platform configuration macro `ZACAS_SUPPORTED`, with a secondary probe by trap-armed execution of amocas; if not satisfied, the whole group TEST_SKIP; Zacas depends on Zaamo (and thus on the A extension), so this group takes the Zaamo probe conclusion of Group 2 as an implicit premise (`ZAAMO_SUPPORTED`/`A_SUPPORTED` must also be satisfied).

**Intersection points with the Hypervisor**:
1. **Exceptions uniformly classified under the store/AMO class**: byte/halfword AMOs (`amoadd.b/h` etc.) and the Zaamo AMOs belong to the same opcode 0x2F instruction family, differing only in funct3=000(.b)/001(.h) versus the 010/011 of word/dword; `norm:mcause_exccode_st_sc_amo` explicitly states "AMO instructions generate store/AMO exceptions", covering all widths, so all their exceptions (misaligned=6, access fault=7, page fault=15, guest-page fault=23) **never** report the load class (4/5/13/21), consistent with the Zaamo conclusion in Group 2; this group re-verifies with the specific byte/halfword instruction words
2. **AMOs require R+W permission (width-independent)**: byte and halfword AMOs to an R=1/W=0 or R=0 page both report a store page-fault (15) (VS-stage) or store guest-page-fault (23) (G-stage); the permission requirement is independent of the access width (.b/.h/.w/.d)
3. **Delegation-path split**: the `hedeleg` bits for VS-stage byte/halfword AMO faults (cause 6/7/15) are Writable and can be delegated down to VS-mode; `hedeleg[23]` for G-stage faults (cause 23) is Read-only 0 and **architecturally forced** to trap into HS-mode (`norm:hedeleg_acc`, `norm:H_vm_gpatrans`) — sharing the same delegation mechanism with Group 1/2/3; this group probes independently to establish the premise (keeping each group self-contained)
4. **An implicit VS-stage walk fault reports cause=23 per the original AMO type**: `norm:H_vm_gpapriv` — an implicit PTE access checks permission as an implicit load/store, but the exception is "always reported for the original access type", so the implicit-walk GPF of a byte/halfword AMO reports a store/AMO guest-page fault (cause=23), consistent with Group 2/3
5. **The htinst transformed atomic instruction must retain the funct3 width encoding (a testable dimension unique to Zabha)**: a byte/halfword AMO explicit-access fault → `htinst`/`mtinst` = 0 or transformed atomic (retaining funct5/aq/rl/**funct3=000(.b)/001(.h)**/rd/rs2, only bits19:15 ← Addr. Offset); funct3 resides at bits14:12 and is retained by `htinst_transformed_atomic` ("all fields ... except bits 19:15"), so HS-mode can determine the **byte/halfword width** of the trapping access from htinst — this is an htinst information dimension unique to Zabha that word/dword AMOs (Group 2) do not have, and it must be confirmed by measurement that the opcode 0x2F branch of the framework `hyp_transform_mem_inst()` correctly retains funct3=000/001
6. **Addr. Offset always 0 (Zabha-specific alignment rationale)**: a byte AMO is **never misaligned** because 1-byte alignment always holds, never splitting; a halfword AMO either raises an exception directly on misalignment (no split) or is MAG-relaxed (a single memory operation); in both cases the faulting VA equals the original VA, hence the htinst Addr. Offset is always 0
7. **MAG relaxation is testable only for halfword AMOs (unique to Zabha)**: the "the same exception options as Zaamo" of `norm:Zabha_rs1_align_addr` cross-references `norm:amo_alignment` and `norm:misaligned_atomicity_granule_size`; but **byte AMOs have no misaligned scenario** (1-byte alignment always holds), so the misaligned-exception and MAG-relaxation branches are **testable only for halfword AMOs (odd addresses)** — this is the key narrowing that distinguishes Zabha from Zaamo (both word/dword can be misaligned)
8. **rd sign-extension to 8/16 bits and ignoring the upper bits of rs2 are unchanged under virtualization (Zabha-specific norms)**: `norm:Zabha_rd_sign_extension` — a byte AMO sign-extends the 8-bit old value into rd and ignores rs2[XLEN-1:8]; a halfword AMO sign-extends the 16-bit old value into rd and ignores rs2[XLEN-1:16]; this data semantics must be consistent with HS-mode in VS/VU-mode
9. **GVA/SPV combination**: a guest byte/halfword AMO trap → GVA=1 and SPV=1 (SPVP=1 for a VS origin, SPVP=0 for a VU origin); contrasted with an HLV.B/HLV.H/HSV.B/HSV.H explicit-access fault → GVA=1 but SPV=0
10. **An AMO always writes ⇒ the D-bit requirement is deterministic**: byte/halfword AMOs always perform a write-back, so when `henvcfg.ADUE`=0 they report a store page-fault (Svade path) for a VS-stage page with A=0 or D=0, **mandatory** (same as HZAMO-27/28 of Group 2, not UNSPECIFIED)
11. **henvcfg.FIOM × aq/rl**: `norm:henvcfg_fiom_order` explicitly states "an atomic instruction ... has its aq and/or rl bit set", covering byte/halfword AMOs; when V=1 and FIOM=1, the ordering of a byte/halfword AMO with aq/rl accessing a device-I/O-ordered region is modified
12. **cause=22 exclusion**: none of the virtual-instruction clauses in `hypervisor.adoc` covers byte/halfword AMOs → guest byte/halfword AMOs never report cause=22 (same as LR/SC/AMO/CAS)
13. **amocas.b/h (Zabha × Zacas intersection)**: if Zacas is implemented, `amocas.b/h` (funct5=00101, funct3=000/001) combines the Zabha width semantics with the Zacas CAS semantics — `norm:Zabha_amocas-BH_ignore_bits` (the comparison uses only rd[7:0]/rd[15:0], ignoring the upper bits) is unchanged in VS/VU-mode; `norm:Zacas_amocas_w_permission` (always requires write permission) makes a **failed CAS** (comparison mismatch) targeting a page without write permission still report a store-class exception (isomorphic to HZACAS-07/12 of Group 3; this group re-verifies with byte/halfword widths)
14. **A reserved byte/halfword lr/sc encoding reports cause=2 rather than cause=22 at V=1 (unique to Zabha)**: the Zabha NOTE explicitly states "omits byte and halfword support for lr and sc", and the LR/SC encodings with funct3=000/001 are reserved encodings; `norm:H_cause_virtual_instruction` stipulates that virtual-instruction (cause=22) replaces **only** instructions that are HS-qualified but blocked at V=1, whereas a reserved encoding is illegal in all modes (including HS/M) (not HS-qualified), so executing it at V=1 reports illegal-instruction (cause=2) rather than cause=22
15. **No byte/halfword atomic virtual-machine equivalent, but HLV.B/HLV.H/HSV.B/HSV.H provide non-atomic memory access (a richer architectural boundary unique to Zabha)**: `norm:hlsv_op` explicitly maps LB/LBU/LH/LHU → HLV.B/HLV.BU/HLV.H/HLV.HU and SB/SH → HSV.B/HSV.H, so HS-mode **can** access guest memory in byte/halfword with VS/VU effective privilege (`norm:hlsv_priv`), but these are **non-atomic** single loads/stores that cannot replace the atomic RMW of a byte/halfword AMO (contrasted with Group 2/3, which only have the word/dword HLV.W/HLV.D counterparts; the byte/halfword counterparts of Zabha are more direct)

**Spec Reference**:
- `norm:Zabha_rd_sign_extension` / `norm:Zabha_amocas-BH_ignore_bits`: the rd sign-extension (8/16 bits) of byte/halfword AMOs and `amocas.b/h` and the ignoring of the upper bits of rs2/rd are unchanged in VS/VU-mode (non-virtualized semantics are covered by `Zabha_test_plan.md`; this group only verifies that virtualization does not change the semantics)

- `zabha_no_byte_halfword_lrsc` / `norm:H_cause_virtual_instruction`: a reserved byte/halfword lr/sc encoding reports illegal-instruction (cause=2) rather than virtual-instruction (cause=22) at V=1

## Group 5. Hypervisor × Zalasr Cross Tests

**Intersection points with the Hypervisor**:
1. **Exception-classification split (load-acquire → load class, store-release → store/AMO class)**: Zalasr provides **independent atomic ordered pure loads and pure stores** (`norm:zalasr_atomic_ordered`); load-acquire (funct5=00110) only reads memory into rd (`norm:ldaq_atomic_load_op`), and store-release (funct5=00111) only writes memory (`norm:sdrl_atomic_store_op`). By functional semantics, load-acquire belongs to the load class (cause 4/5/13/21, same as the LR of Group 1) and store-release to the store/AMO class (cause 6/7/15/23). This is **isomorphic to but mechanistically different from** the LR/SC split of Group 1 Zalrsc (Zalrsc is the two halves of a paired read-modify-write, Zalasr is two independent instructions), and differs from Group 2/3/4 (all read-modify-write AMOs, exceptions uniformly classified under the store/AMO class). **SPEC ambiguity annotation**: the `norm:mcause_exccode_ld_ldrsv`/`norm:mcause_exccode_st_sc_amo` classification text of `machine.adoc` predates Zalasr and does not explicitly name load-acquire/store-release; store-release belongs to the store/AMO class whether treated as a store or an AMO (**unambiguous, mandatorily assertable**), whereas whether load-acquire belongs to the load class or the store/AMO class is ambiguous (if treated as an AMO it would require write permission and report cause 15), so the specific cause class of load-acquire is handled as an **observational recording type** — recording the actual cause and comparing it against the functional classification (load class), reporting deviations to `bugs/` for SPEC clarification, with no arbitrary mandatory verdict
2. **load-acquire requires only read permission (an R=1/W=0 page must execute normally)**: load-acquire is a pure load and **must execute normally** to an R=1/W=0 VS-stage page (contrasted with store-release/AMO requiring write permission and reporting cause 15) — this is the architectural necessity of load-acquire belonging to load semantics (an independent atomic load must be able to read read-only memory, otherwise the "atomic load" of `norm:zalasr_atomic_ordered` loses its meaning); isomorphic to HZLRSC-25 of Group 1 (LR requires only read permission) and HZAMO-09 of Group 2 (LR vs AMO permission contrast)
3. **store-release requires write permission and always writes (the D bit is mandatory)**: store-release is a pure store, **always writing with no "failure" branch** (unlike SC which can fail and CAS which can fail the comparison), so it requires write permission (a W=0 page reports a store page-fault cause 15 / G-stage cause 23), and reporting a store page-fault for a D=0 page when `henvcfg.ADUE`=0 is **mandatory** (same as HZAMO-28 of Group 2, not UNSPECIFIED)
4. **Delegation-path split**: the `hedeleg` bits for VS-stage faults (load-acquire cause 4/5/13, store-release cause 6/7/15) are Writable and can be delegated down to VS-mode; the `hedeleg` bits for G-stage faults (load-acquire cause 21, store-release cause 23) are Read-only 0 and **architecturally forced** to trap into HS-mode (`norm:hedeleg_acc`, `norm:H_vm_gpatrans`) — the load/store split of the delegation path mirrors the exception-class split
5. **An implicit VS-stage walk fault reports per the original type (load-acquire → cause 21, store-release → cause 23)**: `norm:H_vm_gpapriv` — an implicit PTE access checks G-stage permission as an implicit load/store, but the exception is "always reported for the original access type", so the implicit-walk GPF of load-acquire reports a load guest-page fault (cause 21) and store-release reports a store/AMO guest-page fault (cause 23). This implicit-walk cause split is consistent with Group 1 (LR→21, SC→23) and differs from Group 2/3/4 (all AMO→23)
6. **htinst transformed atomic instruction (uniform opcode 0x2F format, not the load/store format)**: although load-acquire/store-release are pure load/store, because they reside in the AMO opcode (0x2F) space and are **not** in the mnemonic lists of `transformedloadinst` (LB..LD, opcode 0x03)/`transformedstoreinst` (SB..SD, opcode 0x23), their `htinst` transformed value uses the `transformedatomicinst` format (retaining all fields except bits19:15, including funct5=00110/00111, aq/rl, funct3, rd/rs2) — this is a **counterintuitive but testable** point: load-acquire is a "load" yet uses the atomic transformed format. The opcode 0x2F branch of the framework `hyp_transform_mem_inst()` already handles this correctly, and HS-mode can distinguish load-acquire(00110)/store-release(00111) by funct5
7. **Addr. Offset always 0**: Zalasr atomicity requires a single memory operation — under MAG relaxation it does not split across pages, and without relaxation it raises an exception directly, hence the htinst Addr. Offset is always 0 (same as Group 2/3/4)
8. **GVA/SPV combination**: a guest load-acquire/store-release trap → GVA=1 and SPV=1 (SPVP=1 for a VS origin, SPVP=0 for a VU origin); contrasted with an HLV/HLVX/HSV explicit-access fault → GVA=1 but SPV=0 (`norm:hstatus_gva_op`/`norm:hstatus_spv_op`)
9. **henvcfg.FIOM × aq/rl (Zalasr instructions always carry aq/rl)**: `norm:henvcfg_fiom_order` explicitly states "an atomic instruction that accesses a region ordered as device I/O has its aq and/or rl bit set". load-acquire always has aq=1 (`norm:ldaq_aq_required`) and store-release always has rl=1 (`norm:sdrl_rl_required`), so when V=1 and FIOM=1 the ordering of a Zalasr instruction accessing a device-I/O-ordered region is **always modified** to constrain both I/O and memory — more universal than Group 1/2/3/4 (aq/rl optional), requiring no construction of specific aq/rl variants
10. **henvcfg.ADUE × A/D update (load-acquire involves only A, store-release involves A+D)**: load-acquire is a pure read, triggering only the A-bit update and **never setting the data-page D bit**; when ADUE=0 it reports a load page-fault (cause 13, Svade path) for an A=0 page. store-release is a pure write, triggering the A+D-bit update; when ADUE=0 it reports a store page-fault (cause 15) for an A=0 or D=0 page. Implicit-walk A/D-update fault: load-acquire → write pseudoinstruction 0x00003020 + cause 21; store-release → write pseudoinstruction 0x00003020 + cause 23 (`norm:H_trap_xtinst_guestpage_rw`)
11. **cause=22 exclusion**: none of the virtual-instruction clauses in `hypervisor.adoc` covers load-acquire/store-release → guest Zalasr never reports cause=22 (same as LR/SC/AMO/CAS)
12. **A reserved encoding reports cause=2 rather than cause=22 at V=1 (same as Zabha)**: a load-acquire without aq (including load-release, `norm:ldaq_no_aq_reserved`) and a store-release without rl (including store-acquire, `norm:sdrl_no_rl_reserved`) are RESERVED; `norm:H_cause_virtual_instruction` stipulates that virtual-instruction (cause=22) replaces only instructions that are HS-qualified but blocked at V=1, whereas a reserved encoding is illegal in all modes (not HS-qualified), so executing it at V=1 reports illegal-instruction (cause=2) rather than cause=22 — isomorphic to HZABHA-37 of Group 4
13. **No atomic ordered load/store virtual-machine equivalent (an architectural boundary, more subtle than AMO)**: `norm:hlsv_op` maps only the RV32I/RV64I basic load/store (opcode 0x03/0x23); load-acquire/store-release (opcode 0x2F) have no HL*/HS* equivalent. But **unlike AMO/CAS**: load-acquire/store-release are themselves pure load/store, so HLV.W can replicate the **data transfer** of load-acquire and HSV.W can replicate the data transfer of store-release — but losing (a) the single-copy atomicity guarantee and (b) the acquire/release RCsc ordering annotations (HLV/HSV have no aq/rl variants). This boundary of "data replicable, atomicity and ordering not replicable" is more subtle than Group 2/3 (the read-modify-write of an AMO cannot even have its data atomically replicated by HLV+HSV)

> - All tests in this group must detect the H extension at runtime via `HAS_H_EXT()`; Zalasr support follows the platform configuration macro `ZALASR_SUPPORTED` (or `ZALASR1P0P0_SUPPORTED`), with a secondary probe by trap-armed execution of load-acquire/store-release; if not satisfied, the whole group TEST_SKIP; **Zalasr can be implemented independently of Zaamo/Zalrsc/Zabha (`norm:zalasr_builds_on_amo`), so its gating does not take the A-extension macros (`ZAAMO_SUPPORTED`/`A_SUPPORTED`) as a precondition** (unlike Group 2/3/4).
> - **The cause classification of load-acquire is an observational recording type (SPEC ambiguity)**: zalasr.adoc does not explicitly stipulate the exception cause classification of load-acquire/store-release (the encodings reside in the AMO opcode 0x2F space); this group designs the expected values by functional semantics (load-acquire is a pure load → load class, store-release is a pure store → store/AMO class), but **for the specific cause of load-acquire (HZLASR-07/12/22/23a/25/31) it adopts the strategy of "record the actual observed value + compare against the functional classification + report deviations to bugs/", with no arbitrary mandatory verdict**; **the cause of store-release (HZLASR-08/09/13/22/23b/26/32) is unambiguous because store and AMO both fall under the store/AMO class, and is a mandatory assertion**.
> - **load-acquire executing normally to an R=1/W=0 page is an architectural necessity (HZLASR-06/10 mandatory positive assertion)**: an independent atomic load must be able to read read-only memory; if the platform requires write permission per AMO semantics and reports cause 15, then load-acquire cannot read a read-only variable, violating the "atomic load" semantics of `norm:zalasr_atomic_ordered`; record the actual cause and report it to `bugs/` as a suspected defect.
> - **cause=22 is a mandatory negative assertion (valid instructions)**: if guest load-acquire/store-release in HZLASR-02/03 report a virtual-instruction exception, it violates the SPEC (no clause in `hypervisor.adoc` imposes virtual-instruction gating on them); it must remain failed and be recorded in the `bugs/` directory, and the assertion must not be relaxed.
> - **A reserved encoding reports cause=2 rather than cause=22 (HZLASR-34 mandatory)**: per `norm:H_cause_virtual_instruction` — virtual-instruction replaces only instructions that are HS-qualified but blocked at V=1, whereas a reserved encoding (a load without aq, a store without rl, including load-release/store-acquire) is illegal in all modes (not HS-qualified), so it reports illegal-instruction (cause=2). If the platform reports cause=22 it violates the SPEC and remains failed.
> - **htinst uniformly uses the opcode 0x2F transformed atomic format**: the framework `hyp_transform_mem_inst()` for opcode 0x2F already implements "Atomic: retain all fields except bits19:15", which in theory covers funct5=00110(load-acquire)/00111(store-release); HZLASR-18~21 must confirm by measuring the actual load-acquire/store-release instruction word that this branch computes the golden value correctly for funct5=00110/00111 (not excluding the possibility that the framework implementation refines handling by funct5; this must be verified by measurement rather than assumed reusable, same as the handling principle of Group 3 HZACAS-17 for funct5=00101); the implicit-walk scenarios of HZLASR-22/23 can reuse `setup_implicit_walk_victim()`.
> - **Mandatory nature of the htinst pseudoinstruction**: in HZLASR-22, when `htval` is non-zero and the fault originates from a VS-stage implicit access, writing 0 to `htinst` violates `norm:H_trap_xtinst_guestpage` ("zero is not allowed") and must remain failed; in the explicit-access scenarios of HZLASR-18/19, writing 0 to `htinst` is allowed.
> - **MAG relaxation branch**: HZLASR-27 depends on the platform declaring a misaligned atomicity granule (`norm:zalasr_misaligned_pma_relax`/`norm:zalasr_misaligned_single_op`); when MAG is not declared, a misaligned load-acquire/store-release must raise an exception (HZLASR-25/26) and HZLASR-27 SKIPs. Misaligned exceptions allow two compliant results — address-misaligned and access-fault; the cases verify that the cause belongs to the expected set and record the actual value, and must not judge FAIL because the platform chose one of them.
> - **store-release always writes ⇒ the D bit is mandatory**: in HZLASR-32 store-release always performs a write, so reporting a store page-fault for a D=0 page when ADUE=0 is a **mandatory** result (not recording type, same as HZAMO-28); load-acquire is a pure read and **does not involve the D bit** (HZLASR-31/33 verify that load-acquire sets only the A bit, not the D bit).
> - **Recording-type cases must not be forcibly judged**: HZLASR-35/36 (the architectural boundary of having no atomic ordered load/store virtual-machine equivalent) are architecture-permitted/implementation-defined behaviors, recording only the implementation choice.
> - **Multi-hart limitation**: the FIOM ordering-effect assertions of HZLASR-29/30 and HZLASR-36 require secondary-hart support; the current common framework runs only hart 0, so the related assertions SKIP (not FAIL) until the framework is ready, and single-hart platforms likewise SKIP with a note. **Spec Reference**:

> [!NOTE]
> - All tests in this group must detect the H extension at runtime via `HAS_H_EXT()` and probe Zawrs support with trap-armed raw encoding (if unimplemented, the whole group TEST_SKIP); Zawrs depends on Zalrsc, and cases must first establish a reservation set with `lr` (the semantics verification of that `lr` belongs to Group 1; this group only uses it as a precondition means).
> - As with the WFI/VTW cases (HSTAT-04), there is a timing dependency: the VTW exception requires that `wrs.nto` "does not complete within the implementation-defined time limit". If the implementation terminates the stall prematurely with a very short duration per `norm:Zawrs_stall_terminate` so that the exception is not raised, the case must remain failed and be recorded in the `bugs/` directory; assertions must not be relaxed.
> - HZWRS-06 is a recording-type case: `norm:vtw_virtinstr` permits the implementation to always raise virtual-instruction when VTW=1 (even if a globally masked pending interrupt exists), so both "completes immediately" and "reports cause=22" are legal implementations; the case only records the implementation choice and constrains the exception type (if raised it must be cause=22), while avoiding any dependency on the implementation's stall duration.
> - HZWRS-08/09 verify the exception type determination: with `mstatus.TW`=1, illegal-instruction (cause=2) must be reported per `norm:Zawrs_priv_illegal_instr_excp`; virtual-instruction must not be reported. Assertions must use precise cause constants.
> - Interrupt environment: the VS/VU cases of this group must execute in a controlled interrupt environment (HZWRS-01~03 and HZWRS-06 need a pre-staged locally enabled pending interrupt; HZWRS-04/05 need all locally enabled interrupts masked), avoiding interrupt delivery polluting trap records.
> - Zawrs non-Hypervisor scenarios (encoding and availability, stall and resume, basic TW behavior) are covered by `Zawrs_test_plan.md`; the basic gating of `hstatus.VTW`/`mstatus.TW` on WFI is covered by `Hypervisor_CSR_test_plan.md` (HSTAT-04/06), and this group does not duplicate them.

## Key Considerations

1. **Extension detection**: All tests must detect the H extension at runtime via `HAS_H_EXT()`; if unavailable, TEST_SKIP. Zalrsc follows the platform configuration macro `ZALRSC_SUPPORTED` with a secondary probe by trap-armed raw encoding; Zaamo follows the platform configuration macro `ZAAMO_SUPPORTED` (or `A_SUPPORTED`) with a secondary probe by trap-armed execution of an AMO; Zacas follows the platform configuration macro `ZACAS_SUPPORTED` with a secondary probe by trap-armed execution of amocas (depends on Zaamo, so `ZAAMO_SUPPORTED`/`A_SUPPORTED` must also be satisfied); Zabha follows the platform configuration macro `ZABHA_SUPPORTED` with a secondary probe by trap-armed execution of a byte/halfword AMO (depends on Zaamo, so `ZAAMO_SUPPORTED`/`A_SUPPORTED` must also be satisfied; its `amocas.b/h` cases additionally require `ZACAS_SUPPORTED`); Zalasr follows the platform configuration macro `ZALASR_SUPPORTED` (or `ZALASR1P0P0_SUPPORTED`) with a secondary probe by trap-armed execution of load-acquire/store-release (**can be implemented independently of Zaamo/Zalrsc/Zabha and does not take the A-extension macros as a precondition**, `norm:zalasr_builds_on_amo`); Zawrs has no independent probe flag and is probed with trap-armed raw encoding (`wrs.nto`=0x00D00073, `wrs.sto`=0x01D00073), with the whole set TEST_SKIP when unimplemented.

2. **Instruction injection convention**: LR/SC are injected with raw encoding if the toolchain does not support the mnemonics (AMO opcode=0x2F, funct5=00010(LR)/00011(SC), funct3=010(.w)/011(.d), bit26=aq, bit25=rl, the rs2 field of LR set to 0); the AMO basic primitives (9 operations × .w/.d) are already provided by `common/mem_ops.h`, and the aq/rl variants are injected with raw encoding if primitives are missing (opcode=0x2F, funct5: amoadd=00000/amoswap=00001/amoxor=00100/amoor=00110/amoand=00111/amomin=01000/amomax=01001/amominu=01100/amomaxu=01101); amocas is injected with raw encoding if the toolchain (`-march` including `zacas`) does not support the mnemonics (opcode=0x2F, funct5=00101, funct3=010(.w)/011(.d)/100(.q), bit26=aq, bit25=rl), and `amocas.d` on RV32 / `amocas.q` on RV64 must use even register pairs (rd/rd+1, rs2/rs2+1); byte/halfword AMOs are injected with raw encoding if the toolchain (`-march` including `zabha`) does not support the mnemonics (opcode=0x2F, funct5 same as word AMO, **funct3=000(.b)/001(.h)**, bit26=aq, bit25=rl), `amocas.b/h` is funct5=00101 + funct3=000/001, and reserved byte/halfword lr/sc is funct5=00010(lr)/00011(sc) + funct3=000/001; load-acquire/store-release are injected with raw encoding if the toolchain (`-march` including `zalasr`) does not support the mnemonics (opcode=0x2F, load-acquire funct5=00110 with the rs2 field set to 0 and aq(bit26)=1, store-release funct5=00111 with the rd field set to 0 and rl(bit25)=1, funct3=000(.b)/001(.h)/010(.w)/011(.d, RV64)), and reserved encodings are load funct5=00110+aq=0 / store funct5=00111+rl=0; wrs instructions are always injected with raw encoding (SYSTEM opcode=0x73). All the above instructions uniformly use `.option norvc` to guarantee a 4-byte instruction length, so the trap handler can skip the faulting instruction by sepc+4.

3. **Group 1 Zalrsc cross key points**:
    - **Exception class and cause split**: LR is a load access (cause 4/5/13/21) and SC is a store access (cause 6/7/15/23), per `norm:mcause_exccode_st_sc_amo`, `norm:load_page_fault_no_r`, `norm:store_page_fault_no_w`. Assertions must use precise cause constants, must not confuse the two classes, and must not misjudge a VS-stage fault (13/15) as a G-stage fault (21/23).
    - **Delegation-path split**: `hedeleg` bit 4/5/6/7/13/15 are writable (VS-stage faults can be delegated down to VS-mode), bit 21/23 are read-only zero (G-stage faults are architecturally forced to trap into HS-mode). HZLRSC-05 probes the bit attributes first to establish the premise of subsequent cases.
    - **htinst two-path disambiguation**: an explicit-access fault → transformed atomic (retaining funct5/aq/rl/funct3/rd/rs2, only bits19:15 ← Addr. Offset) or 0; an implicit VS-stage page-table-walk fault → **must** be a pseudoinstruction (read 0x00003000 / A-D write 0x00003020, RV64) and 0 is not allowed. Writing 0 for the latter violates `norm:H_trap_xtinst_guestpage` and must remain failed.
    - **A failed SC still reports a store-class exception** (the point most easily overlooked by implementations): `norm:sc_retire_permission` is mandatory (shall), so an SC with no reservation that logically does not write memory must still report cause=15 (VS-stage) or cause=23 (G-stage) when targeting a page without write permission (HZLRSC-23/24).
    - **cause=22 negative assertion**: no clause in `hypervisor.adoc` imposes virtual-instruction gating on LR/SC; guest LR/SC reporting cause=22 violates the SPEC, and the assertion must not be relaxed.
    - **No MAG relaxation on misalignment**: Zalrsc differs from Zalasr — a misaligned LR/SC must raise an exception, with no "atomic completion without exception within the same MAG" branch; and it does not split the access, so `stval`=the misaligned address itself and the htinst Addr. Offset is always 0. Both address-misaligned and access-fault results are compliant, and the case must not judge FAIL because the platform chose one of them.
    - **Recording-type case list**: HZLRSC-32/33 (the PTE D-bit side effect of a failed SC, double UNSPECIFIED for VS-stage/G-stage), HZLRSC-37 (unconstrained sequence), HZLRSC-38 (reservation survival after a trap round-trip), HZLRSC-39/40 (the no-HLR/HSC architectural boundary) record only the implementation choice and must not be forcibly judged.
    - **Framework readiness**: `hyp_transform_mem_inst()` (opcode 0x2F branch) already provides the transformed golden-value computation for LR/SC, and `setup_implicit_walk_victim()` already provides the implicit-walk fault construction capability, so HZLRSC-15~21 require no new infrastructure.

4. **Group 2 Zaamo cross key points**:
    - **Exceptions uniformly classified under the store/AMO class** (the fundamental difference from LR): even though an AMO includes a read phase, all its exceptions are classified under the store/AMO class (misaligned=6, access fault=7, page fault=15, guest-page fault=23), never reporting the load class (4/5/13/21), per `norm:mcause_exccode_st_sc_amo` and the `norm:store_page_fault_no_w` accompanying NOTE (an AMO to an unreadable page also reports cause=15 rather than 13).
    - **AMOs require R+W permission**: an AMO to an R=1/W=0 or R=0 page reports cause=15 (VS-stage) / cause=23 (G-stage); HZAMO-09 establishes this divide by contrasting LR (requires only R, executes normally) with AMO (reports 15).
    - **An implicit-walk fault reports cause=23**: `norm:H_vm_gpapriv` stipulates that an implicit VS-stage access is reported per the original type, so the implicit PTE-walk GPF of an AMO reports cause=23 (HZAMO-18), unlike the cause=21 of LR (HZLRSC-19).
    - **MAG relaxation is unique to Zaamo**: HZAMO-22/23 depend on the platform declaring MAG; when it is not declared, a misaligned AMO must report cause=6/7 (HZAMO-21) and HZAMO-22/23 SKIP; contrasted with Zalrsc which has no MAG relaxation (HZLRSC-27/28).
    - **An AMO always writes ⇒ the D bit is mandatory**: HZAMO-28 (ADUE=0 + D=0 → cause=15) is a mandatory result, unlike the UNSPECIFIED D-bit side effect of a failed SC (HZLRSC-32/33, recording type).
    - **Framework readiness**: `hyp_transform_mem_inst()` (opcode 0x2F branch) already provides the transformed golden-value computation for AMOs, and `common/mem_ops.h` already provides the `.w`/`.d` primitives of the 9 AMOs; the aq/rl variants (HZAMO-16/25) and implicit-walk construction (HZAMO-18/19, reusing `setup_implicit_walk_victim()`) must be supplemented if primitives are missing.
    - **Recording-type case list**: HZAMO-30/31 (the architectural boundary of having no AMO virtual-machine equivalent) record only the implementation choice and must not be forcibly judged.

5. **Group 3 Zacas cross key points**:
    - **Exceptions uniformly classified under the store/AMO class** (consistent with Zaamo): amocas belongs to the AMO instruction family (opcode 0x2F, funct5=00101), and all its exceptions are classified under the store/AMO class (misaligned=6, access fault=7, page fault=15, guest-page fault=23), never reporting the load class (4/5/13/21), on the same basis as Group 2 (`norm:mcause_exccode_st_sc_amo`).
    - **The unconditional write-permission check is a Zacas-specific mandatory dimension**: `norm:Zacas_amocas_w_permission` ("always requires write permissions") does not distinguish comparison success/failure, so HZACAS-07/12 (a failed CAS still reports cause=15/23) are **mandatory** verdicts, isomorphic to HZLRSC-22~26 of Group 1 (failed-SC permission check), and unlike Zaamo in Group 2 which has no "failure" branch (an AMO always writes back unconditionally).
    - **The D-bit side effect of a failed CAS is a SPEC gap (recording type)**: HZACAS-32/33 are handled as recording type because the Zacas SPEC does not explicitly stipulate UNSPECIFIED the way Zalrsc's `norm:sc_failed_side_effects` does, with no mandatory verdict, and the specification difference must be explicitly annotated (unlike HZLRSC-32/33 which have an explicit basis, and HZAMO-28 which is a mandatory result).
    - **An implicit-walk fault reports cause=23**: same as Group 2, `norm:H_vm_gpapriv` stipulates that an implicit VS-stage access is reported per the original type, so the implicit PTE-walk GPF of amocas reports cause=23 (HZACAS-20).
    - **MAG relaxation applies via cross-reference**: HZACAS-24/25 depend on the platform declaring MAG (via the "the same exception options apply" cross-reference of `norm:Zacas_amocas_rs1_addr_alignment` to `norm:misaligned_atomicity_granule_size`); when it is not declared, a misaligned amocas must report cause=6/7 (HZACAS-23) and HZACAS-24/25 SKIP.
    - **Framework readiness**: `hyp_transform_mem_inst()` (opcode 0x2F branch) in theory covers funct5=00101 (amocas), but HZACAS-17~19 must confirm by measurement that the golden-value computation is correct, and it must not be assumed reusable; the aq/rl variants (HZACAS-18/27) and implicit-walk construction (HZACAS-20/21, reusing `setup_implicit_walk_victim()`) must be supplemented if primitives are missing.
    - **Recording-type case list**: HZACAS-32/33 (the PTE D-bit side effect of a failed CAS, a SPEC gap) and HZACAS-34/35 (the architectural boundary of having no amocas virtual-machine equivalent) record only the implementation choice and must not be forcibly judged.

6. **Group 4 Zabha cross key points**:
    - **Exceptions uniformly classified under the store/AMO class (width-independent)**: byte/halfword AMOs and `amocas.b/h` belong to the AMO instruction family (opcode 0x2F, funct3=000/001), and all their exceptions are classified under the store/AMO class (misaligned=6, access fault=7, page fault=15, guest-page fault=23), never reporting the load class (4/5/13/21), on the same basis as Group 2/3 (`norm:mcause_exccode_st_sc_amo`); the permission requirement is independent of the access width (HZABHA-09 establishes this by all three of .b/.h/.w reporting cause=15).
    - **htinst funct3 width retention is a Zabha-specific mandatory dimension**: HZABHA-16/35 must confirm by measurement that the opcode 0x2F branch of the framework `hyp_transform_mem_inst()` correctly retains funct3=000(.b)/001(.h) (bits14:12), so HS-mode can determine the trapping access width from htinst; it must not be assumed that sharing the branch with word/dword AMOs makes the width encoding automatically correct.
    - **Byte AMOs have no misaligned scenario (distinct from Zaamo)**: HZABHA-22 verifies that a byte AMO has 1-byte alignment always holding, completes normally at any address, and never reports cause=6/7; the misaligned-exception and MAG-relaxation branches (HZABHA-23~26, HZABHA-36) are testable only for halfword AMOs (odd addresses).
    - **rd sign-extension and rs2/rd upper-bit ignoring are unchanged under virtualization**: HZABHA-04/32 verify that `norm:Zabha_rd_sign_extension` (rd sign-extends 8/16 bits, ignoring the upper bits of rs2) and `norm:Zabha_amocas-BH_ignore_bits` (amocas.b/h ignores the upper bits of rd in the comparison) are consistent with HS-mode in VS/VU-mode.
    - **A failed CAS still requires a write-permission check (amocas.b/h)**: HZABHA-33 rests on the "always" unconditional language of `norm:Zacas_amocas_w_permission`; a successful/failed CAS to an R=1/W=0 page both report cause=15, isomorphic to HZACAS-07/12 of Group 3 (this group re-verifies with byte/halfword widths).
    - **A reserved byte/halfword lr/sc reports cause=2 rather than cause=22**: HZABHA-37 rests on `norm:H_cause_virtual_instruction` — a reserved encoding is not HS-qualified and still reports illegal-instruction (cause=2) at V=1; reporting cause=22 violates the SPEC.
    - **An AMO always writes ⇒ the D bit is mandatory**: HZABHA-30 (ADUE=0 + D=0 → cause=15) is a mandatory result, same as HZAMO-28.
    - **Framework readiness**: `hyp_transform_mem_inst()` (opcode 0x2F branch) must be confirmed by measurement with HZABHA-16/35 that the golden-value computation for funct3=000/001 is correct; if `common/mem_ops.h` provides only `.w`/`.d` primitives, the byte/halfword primitives and aq/rl variants (HZABHA-17/27) must be supplemented or injected with raw encoding; the implicit-walk construction (HZABHA-19/20) reuses `setup_implicit_walk_victim()`.
    - **Recording-type case list**: HZABHA-38/39 (the architectural boundary of having no byte/halfword atomic virtual-machine equivalent) record only the implementation choice and must not be forcibly judged.

7. **Group 5 Zalasr cross key points**:
    - **Exception-classification split (load-acquire → load class, store-release → store/AMO class)**: load-acquire is a pure load (`norm:ldaq_atomic_load_op`) functionally belonging to the load class (cause 4/5/13/21, requires only read permission), and store-release is a pure store (`norm:sdrl_atomic_store_op`) belonging to the store/AMO class (cause 6/7/15/23, requires write permission); isomorphic to but mechanistically different from the LR/SC split of Group 1 (Zalrsc is the two halves of a paired read-modify-write, Zalasr is two independent instructions), and unlike Group 2/3/4 (all AMOs uniformly classified under the store/AMO class).
    - **The cause classification of load-acquire is an observational recording type (SPEC ambiguity)**: zalasr.adoc does not explicitly stipulate the cause classification of load-acquire/store-release (the encodings reside in the AMO opcode space), so **the specific cause of load-acquire (HZLASR-07/12/22/23a/25/31) adopts the strategy of "record the actual observed value + compare against the functional classification (load class) + report deviations to bugs/", with no arbitrary mandatory verdict**; **the cause of store-release (HZLASR-08/09/13/22/23b/26/32) is unambiguous because store and AMO both fall under the store/AMO class, and is a mandatory assertion**.
    - **load-acquire executing normally to an R=1/W=0 page is an architectural necessity**: HZLASR-06/10 mandatory positive assertion — an independent atomic load must be able to read read-only memory; if the platform reports cause 15 per AMO semantics it violates `norm:zalasr_atomic_ordered`, and it is recorded and reported to `bugs/`.
    - **An implicit-walk fault reports per the original type (load-acquire→21, store-release→23)**: `norm:H_vm_gpapriv`, consistent with Group 1 (LR→21, SC→23), unlike Group 2/3/4 (all AMO→23).
    - **htinst uniformly uses the opcode 0x2F transformed atomic format**: although load-acquire/store-release are pure load/store, they use `transformedatomicinst` (not transformedload/storeinst); HZLASR-18~21 must confirm by measurement that the framework `hyp_transform_mem_inst()` computes the golden value correctly for funct5=00110/00111 (same measurement principle as Group 3 for funct5=00101); HS-mode can distinguish load/store by funct5.
    - **FIOM is always applicable (Zalasr always carries aq/rl)**: load-acquire always has aq=1 and store-release always has rl=1, so the ordering modification of `norm:henvcfg_fiom_order` is always applicable to Zalasr (HZLASR-29/30), requiring no construction of specific aq/rl variants (unlike Group 1/2/3/4).
    - **store-release always writes ⇒ the D bit is mandatory; load-acquire is a pure read and does not involve D**: HZLASR-32 (ADUE=0 + D=0 → cause 15) is mandatory (same as HZAMO-28); HZLASR-31/33 verify that load-acquire sets only the A bit, not the D bit.
    - **A reserved encoding reports cause=2 rather than cause=22**: HZLASR-34 rests on `norm:H_cause_virtual_instruction` (a reserved load-without-aq / store-without-rl encoding is not HS-qualified), same as HZABHA-37.
    - **Gating independent of the A extension**: Zalasr can be implemented independently (`norm:zalasr_builds_on_amo`), and its gating depends only on `ZALASR_SUPPORTED`, not taking `ZAAMO_SUPPORTED`/`A_SUPPORTED` as a precondition (unlike Group 2/3/4).
    - **Framework readiness**: `hyp_transform_mem_inst()` (opcode 0x2F branch) must be confirmed by measurement with HZLASR-18~21 for the golden value of funct5=00110/00111; if `common/mem_ops.h` has no load-acquire/store-release primitives they must be supplemented or injected with raw encoding; the implicit-walk construction (HZLASR-22/23) reuses `setup_implicit_walk_victim()`.
    - **Recording-type case list**: HZLASR-35/36 (the architectural boundary of having no atomic ordered load/store virtual-machine equivalent) record only the implementation choice and must not be forcibly judged.

8. **Group 6 Zawrs timing dependency**: the `wrs.nto` cases depend on the timing condition of "does not complete within the implementation-defined time limit" (same style as the WFI/VTW case HSTAT-04 of `Hypervisor_CSR_test_plan.md`). If the implementation terminates the stall prematurely per `norm:Zawrs_stall_terminate`, the case must remain failed and be recorded in `bugs/`; assertions must not be relaxed. HZWRS-06 is recording type (`norm:vtw_virtinstr` permits two legal behaviors).

9. **Distinguishing virtual-instruction from illegal-instruction** (common to multiple groups): LR/SC in Group 1, AMOs in Group 2, amocas in Group 3, byte/halfword AMOs and amocas.b/h in Group 4, and load-acquire/store-release in Group 5 must **never** report cause=22 (negative assertion; no clause in `hypervisor.adoc` imposes virtual-instruction gating on atomic instructions); the reserved byte/halfword lr/sc encodings in Group 4 and the reserved load-acquire (without aq)/store-release (without rl) encodings in Group 5 **must** report illegal-instruction (cause=2) rather than cause=22 because they are not HS-qualified (`norm:H_cause_virtual_instruction`); `wrs.nto` in Group 6 **must** report cause=22 when VTW=1/TW=0 and **must** report cause=2 when TW=1 (positive assertions). Assertions must use precise cause constants and must not confuse them.

10. **Multi-hart limitation**: the FIOM ordering-effect assertions of HZLRSC-30/31, HZAMO-25/26, HZACAS-27/28, HZABHA-27/28, and HZLASR-29/30, and HZLRSC-40, HZAMO-31, HZACAS-35, HZABHA-39, and HZLASR-36 require secondary-hart support; the current common framework runs only hart 0, so the related assertions SKIP (not FAIL) until the framework is ready, and single-hart platforms likewise SKIP with a note.

11. **Failure handling**: on any platform, an LR/SC exception-class error (LR reporting the store class or SC reporting the load class), an AMO/amocas/byte-halfword-AMO exception-class error (reporting the load class 4/5/13/21, or an implicit-walk GPF reporting cause=21 rather than 23), **a store-release exception-class error (reporting the load class 4/5/13/21, or an implicit-walk GPF reporting cause=21 rather than 23), or load-acquire to an R=1/W=0 page reporting a store page-fault (violating pure-load semantics, HZLASR-06/10; the remaining cause classification of load-acquire is reported as an observational record due to the SPEC ambiguity rather than directly judged failed)**, a guest-page fault not forced to trap into HS-mode, an htinst pseudoinstruction writing 0 or the funct3 width encoding not retained (HZABHA-16/35), a failed SC or failed CAS (including amocas.b/h) not performing the write-permission check (the mandatory-assertion failures corresponding to HZLRSC-23/24, HZACAS-07/12, HZABHA-33), guest LR/SC/AMO/amocas/byte-halfword-AMO/load-acquire/store-release reporting cause=22, a reserved byte/halfword lr/sc encoding or reserved load-acquire/store-release encoding reporting cause=22 rather than cause=2 (HZABHA-37, HZLASR-34), a constrained-loop livelock, or a Zawrs VTW/TW exception-type determination error, are all implementation defects violating the SPEC; the case remains FAIL and is recorded in the `bugs/` directory (including the platform, instruction, address, expected/actual cause, and CSR context), and it is strictly forbidden to circumvent it by skipping or a workaround.
